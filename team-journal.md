Your team will keep a journal of your research and learning activities each time you sit down to work on your project. Some people like paper journals or lab books. 

The journal is your team's log of what happened. It records each team member's progress for the day: what they did, what problems were encountered and solved, any interesting things you observed. It's also a place to reflect your own process and explore issues such as what's going well, what isn't, and how you can improve.  While not every team member must work on the project everyday, your team as a whole should have a pattern of consistent progress throughout each week.

The journal is not a formal document. As such, I don't expect perfect grammar, and it may get messy at times.  However, it should be an organized mess.  Days and people should be clearly labeled and chronology should be preserved.
